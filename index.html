Snapshot 02: July 2026


I just joined HackClub through the Stardance challenge. This year, I really want to grow as a person and actually do things I can look back on — things that show my personality and interests.

I’ve always had a strong curiosity for technology. As I start thinking about post‑secondary school, I know I want to study something in STEM. Engineering is at the top of my list. I’ve done a few projects before — programming a Pololu Zumo Robot with an autonomous line‑reading program, building two car robots, and making several attempts at websites — but I never documented any of them. None of those projects feel like something I can point to and say, “I made this for myself.”

That’s why building a macropad for the Hackpad mission was so exciting. It was my first time designing a PCB, and I genuinely feel like the project improved my CAD design and coding skills. I can’t wait to do more HackClub projects. Their KEEB YSWS program looks really interesting…
